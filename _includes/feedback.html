Master Bow Tuning

A bow out of spec wastes kinetic energy and masks your true shooting ability. I bring your equipment back into absolute harmony.

  • Factory Spec Reset: Checking axle-to-axle, brace height, and cam lean.
  • Timing & Synchronization: Ensuring cams roll over perfectly together.
  • Paper & Lasers Tuning: Eliminating tail-left or tail-right tear for clean flight.
  • Rest & Sight Setup: Precise third-axis leveling for long-range accuracy.

Custom Arrow Builds

Stop shooting generic, pre-fletched arrows off a retail rack. Truly consistent groups require arrows built specifically for your draw weight, length, and cam system.

  • Spine Matching: Calculated perfectly to your bow's unique power stroke.
  • Precision Squaring: Squaring both ends of the shaft for flawless component alignment.
  • Weight Sorting: Sifting components down to the grain for tight velocity spreads.
  • Custom Fletching: Helical configurations tailored for optimal broadhead control.
